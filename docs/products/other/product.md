# Neck Hammock Neck Stretcher Head Hammock

## Description

The Neck Hammock Neck Stretcher is a portable cervical traction accessory designed to support gentle neck relaxation and stretching. It attaches to a standard door and creates a simple suspension setup that can be used at home, in the office, or while traveling.

Compact and easy to store, it offers a straightforward way to add a short decompression routine to the day. The soft, cushioned construction helps keep the contact points comfortable during use.
